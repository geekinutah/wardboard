- `a namesake stays unresolved until a candidate or a new category is chosen` — missing category stays unresolved; a candidate match is the only resolution; an unknown key is ignored; youth new is ready.
- `a conflict cannot be matched` — `report_conflict` has no match options and needs a category; match and bare new stay unresolved; youth new is ready.
- `a returning member stays unresolved until return is confirmed` — returning rows follow decision rows that already need a choice; match without `confirmReturn` is not ready; confirmed return plus the other match is ready; a wrong id is not ready.
- `a duplicate report keeps replace off` — when both duplicate and inconsistent are set, blocked is `duplicate_report`; source statuses follow the duplicate keys; a resolved identity still leaves replace off.
- `disagreeing reports keep replace off` — inconsistent reports with no rows leave unresolved at 0 and replace off.
- `a category-only new person needs adult or youth` — a new person already categorized and absent from `needsCategory` is not a row; a categorized person still listed in `needsCategory` must choose adult or youth.
- `a resemblance keeps the known category on treat-as-new` — resemblance with a known category does not require a new category.
- `preview posts the three pdf fields` — POST `/api/import/preview`, `credentials: 'same-origin'`, JSON, standard base64 with no `data:` prefix.
- `replace sends the version and the resolutions` — POST `/api/import/replace` with `expectedVersion` from the caller.
- `a forbidden preview is not a preview` — 401/403 are `forbidden` even if the body contains `decisions`.
- `a stale replace names the current version` — 409 `stale` keeps a finite `currentVersion`.
- `an offline preview is offline` — a thrown or rejected fetch is `offline`.
- `a too-large preview is too_large` — 413 is `too_large`.
- `files become base64 without a data url` — `readReportFiles` matches standard base64, including bytes above 127.
- `the review hides the replace action until every identity is resolved` — heading stays `Needs your decision · 2 identities`; the button moves from `Resolve 2 identities to continue` to `Resolve 1 identity to continue` to `Replace Current`; one replace call uses version 3 and the chosen resolutions; success text is `Replaced Current. - `choosing adult or youth is visible on the namesake row` — Adult and Youth appear after Treat as new on the namesake; the already-adult row does not show them.
- `discarded fields stay visible as text` — Phone, Email, Address, Birth date, Age, Record number, Class rosters, Ministering.
- `a failed replace does not announce success` — `unresolved` shows `Replace did not run (unresolved).`; stale shows `Current changed. It is now version 9.`; the success line stays absent.
- `no identities leaves replace enabled` — zero rows, `Replace Current` enabled, resolutions `{}`.
- `a blocked preview tells why replace stays off` — duplicate and inconsistent alerts; button stays `Replace Current` and disabled.
- `the preview shows the review and does not replace on render`.
- Playwright `import preview resolves identities before replace` on desktop-chromium, phone-chromium, phone-webkit, and tv-chromium: no `/api/import` traffic, steps, sources, stats, discarded labels, resolution flow, success line, no horizontal overflow, every import button at least 44px.
